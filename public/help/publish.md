# Publish and update

Publishing makes a story readable by anyone with its link. Until you publish, only you and the people you've shared it with can open it.

## Publish a story

1. In the story editor, select **Publish**.
2. Choose the end of the link. Waymark suggests one from the title. Use 3 to 60 lowercase letters, numbers and dashes, such as `river-of-change`. The full address is shown, for example `https://stories.example.org/s/river-of-change`.
3. Select **Publish**.

The dialog shows the live link with **Copy** and open buttons. When the link is shared on social media or in chat apps, it shows the story's title, subtitle and cover photo.

> [!NOTE]
> Only the story's owner can publish or unpublish it. Editors can change the draft; the owner publishes those changes.

## What readers see

Readers see the **published copy**: the story as it was the last time you selected **Publish**. Your edits go into the **draft**, which saves continuously. This lets you work on a live story without readers seeing half-finished changes.

When the draft has changes readers haven't seen, the editor's Publish dialog says **You have edits readers haven't seen yet**, and the card on the home screen shows **Unpublished edits**.

## Update a published story

1. Make your changes in the editor.
2. Select **Publish**, then **Publish changes** in the dialog.

Readers get the new version the next time they open or reload the story. Saved maps are the exception: a published story always loads the latest version of the saved maps it uses, so edits to a saved map reach readers without republishing. See [Use a saved map](story-maps.md#use-a-saved-map).

## Change the link

Open **Publish**, select **Unpublish**, then publish again with the new link. The old link stops working, so update anywhere you've shared it.

## Unpublish

Open **Publish** and select **Unpublish**. The link stops working immediately and the story becomes a draft again. Nothing is deleted; publish again whenever you like.

## Share a published story

| To… | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Send the link | Copy it from the Publish dialog, or use **Copy link** in the card's **⋯** menu on the home screen. |
| Put the story inside another website | Use the embed code under **Share…** in the card's menu. See [Embed in a website](sharing.md#embed-in-a-website). |
| Show it on your public page | Tick **Show it on my public page** under **Share…**. See [Your public page](sharing.md#your-public-page). |
| Let colleagues edit it | Share it with them as editors. See [Share with people and groups](sharing.md#share-with-people-and-groups). |
| Host it on your own web server | Export it as a website. See [Export a story as a website](export.md#export-a-story-as-a-website). |

## Preview before publishing

- **Preview** in the top bar opens the draft in a new tab, exactly as readers will see it.
- The phone button above the preview shows the story at phone width.
- **Preview draft** in the card's menu on the home screen does the same without opening the editor.

People you've shared the story with can open the draft preview too. Nobody else can.

## Duplicate or delete a story

On the home screen, open the story card's **⋯** menu:

- **Duplicate** makes a private copy titled "(copy)", which you can change freely.
- **Delete** removes the story permanently. If it's published, its link stops working. Only the owner can delete a story. Saved maps and hosted layers the story uses are not deleted.
